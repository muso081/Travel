Privacy Policy

What TravelTogether keeps, where it is kept, which other services are involved, and how to get it deleted. Written in plain words.

The short version

  • No account needed. Without one, everything you plan stays in this browser on this device.
  • With an account, your email, a display name and your plans are stored in our database so you can open them on any device.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking cookies, and we never sell or share your data for marketing.
  • You can delete it. Clear the site's data in your browser, and ask us to delete your account.

TravelTogether is a student team project for the Web Programming course at Gachon University. It is not a company and does not sell anything.

Kept in your browser

The site saves what you do in your browser's local storage, which stays on your device. It holds:

  • your trips: names, destinations, dates, number of travelers and currency;
  • each trip's itinerary, budget and expenses, and packing list;
  • destinations you marked as favourites, and your light or dark theme;
  • a saved copy of the destination list, so Explore opens quickly;
  • if you log in, a sign-in token so you stay logged in.

This is not a cookie and it is not sent anywhere on its own. Anyone using the same browser profile can see it, so log out on shared computers.

If you create an account

Accounts are optional. When you sign up we store:

  • your email address and password. The password is checked and stored by our sign-in provider, Supabase, in hashed form. We cannot read it.
  • a display name, if you give one.
  • your plans: the trips, itineraries, budgets and packing lists listed above, so they appear on every device you log in on.
  • whether you are an admin, which only the project team can change.

We use this only to run your account and show your plans back to you. The database is set up so that each account can read and change only its own plans.

Destinations and photos

Admins can add and edit destinations. Those destinations, including any photo an admin uploads, are public: every visitor sees them. The database also notes which admin account created each one. Please don't upload photos that show private people or private information.

Other services involved

GitHub Pages
Hosts the site. Like any web host, GitHub receives your IP address and browser details when you load a page. See GitHub's privacy statement.
Supabase
Runs the accounts and the database described above. See Supabase's privacy policy.
Wikipedia (Wikimedia)
Only for a destination an admin added without its own photo: your browser asks Wikipedia for a photo of that place, so Wikimedia sees your IP address and the place name. See Wikimedia's privacy policy.

There are no other third parties: no advertising networks, analytics tools or social media trackers.

How long it is kept

  • In your browser: until you delete it, clear the site's data, or log out. Logging out removes your synced plans from that browser, but they stay in your account.
  • In your account: until you delete the plans or ask us to delete the account. When the course project ends, we will delete the database.

Your choices

  • Use the site without an account. Nothing about you leaves your device, apart from the hosting and photo requests above.
  • Download your plans at any time with the Export buttons on the Itinerary, Budget and Packing pages.
  • Delete trips, activities, expenses and items yourself. Deleting them while logged in deletes them from your account too.
  • Ask to see, correct or delete everything stored about your account (see Contact).

Security

The site and the database are only reached over encrypted HTTPS connections, and database rules keep each account's plans private to that account. No system is perfectly secure, so please don't put sensitive things like passport numbers, card details or health information into trip names, notes or packing lists.

Children

TravelTogether is not meant for children under 14, and we do not knowingly create accounts for them. If you think a child has made an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top. For questions, or to have your account deleted, contact the TravelTogether team through the project's GitHub page. Please don't post your email address or other personal details in a public issue: say that you'd like to be contacted, and we will reply privately.
