What TravelTogether is

TravelTogether is a free travel planner made as a student team project for the Web Programming course at Gachon University. It helps a group keep one trip's itinerary, budget and packing list in one place. It is provided as it is, for planning and learning, and is not a commercial service.

Sample prices, no bookings

  • Prices are examples. Daily costs, plan prices, flights and places to stay shown on the site are rounded, illustrative figures. They are not live prices or offers.
  • Nothing is booked or paid for. Choosing a plan only fills in your itinerary and budget. No ticket, room or payment is made, and the site never asks for card details.
  • Check before you travel. Opening hours, visa and entry rules, health advice and safety can change. Always confirm them with official sources and the companies you book with.

Accounts

You don't need an account. If you make one, use a real email address you can access, keep your password to yourself, and tell us if you think someone else has used your account. One account is for one person. We may suspend or delete accounts that break these terms or put the site at risk.

Your plans

The trips, notes, expenses and lists you create are yours. You let us store them and show them back to you, which is all we use them for (see the Privacy Policy). Please don't use the site to store anything illegal or anything that belongs to someone else without permission.

Admins and photos

Admins can add, edit and remove destinations, and everything they add is shown to every visitor. An admin may only upload a photo they took or have permission to use. Photos fetched from Wikipedia stay under their own licences and are credited with a link to their Wikipedia page.

If you own a photo or text shown here and want it removed, contact us and we will take it down.

Fair use

Please don't try to break into accounts or the database, get around the admin rules, overload the site with automated requests, or use it to harass anyone or spread harmful content. The source code is public on GitHub. You're welcome to read it, learn from it and suggest improvements there.

Availability

We may change features, reset sample data, or take the site down, for example when the course ends. Keep your own copy of anything important with the Export buttons on the Itinerary, Budget and Packing pages.

No guarantees

The site is offered free and without warranties. We do our best to keep it working and accurate, but we can't promise it will always be available, error-free, or right about any destination. As far as the law allows, the team is not responsible for losses that come from relying on it, including travel costs, missed bookings or lost data.

Changes and contact

If these terms change, we will update this page and the date at the top. Questions? Contact the TravelTogether team through the project's GitHub page.
